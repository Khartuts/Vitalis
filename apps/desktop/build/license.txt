VITALIS — Personal AI Health Intelligence

VITALIS is personal wellness software. It is NOT a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any
disease. Wearable measurements and VITALIS estimates can be inaccurate. Consult a qualified healthcare
professional about health concerns.

Your data stays on this computer in an encrypted database. Optional cloud features (AI providers, Google Health)
only run after you connect them, and every AI request is shown to you before it is sent.

Third-party components are distributed under their own licenses (see resources/engine/python/LICENSE.txt and
the licenses of the bundled Python packages and Electron/Chromium).
